## Safety checks

- Serious neurological/spinal DCI and suspected AGE activate hyperbaric consultation immediately. Oxygen and stabilization occur in parallel. Routine MRI is not a prerequisite for indicated recompression.
- A negative acute MRI is not treated as exclusion of spinal DCS. True traumatic/compressive alternatives retain urgent targeted evaluation.
- Pulmonary barotrauma is considered. Clinical tension receives immediate treatment, and confirmed pneumothorax requires drainage before recompression. Chest investigations are selective rather than a universal delay.
- High-concentration oxygen is not limited by normal saturation or stopped because symptoms improve. Fluid advice uses perfusion reassessment with pulmonary caution and retains emergency hypoglycemia treatment.
- Positioning prioritizes airway and breathing, avoids Trendelenburg, and prohibits self-directed underwater treatment.
- Apparently mild cases require specialist assessment. The text does not convert transient resolution of serious symptoms into mild disease or automatic discharge.
- The steroid/antithrombotic caution is restricted to empiric DCI treatment. Separate indications, including inpatient thromboprophylaxis, are explicitly preserved.
- Transport requires a capable accepting service, adequate oxygen, serial findings and individualized altitude planning. Return-to-diving/flying advice is not a fixed clearance rule.
- The only treatment concentration is oxygen as close to 100% as feasible; no unvalidated dose, severity score or chamber-pressure schedule is introduced. DAN emergency number matches CDC.
