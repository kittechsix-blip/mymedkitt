# Decompression sickness outline: independent clinical review

Date: 2026-11-17
Reviewer: independent AI-assisted review using the Dr. Kitlowski clinical-audit playbook. This is not human physician sign-off.
Scope: proposed outline only, not implemented app behavior, calculations, or deployment.

## Verdict

PASS for proposal scope. The pneumothorax wording clarification below was applied and independently rechecked. No app changes were made by this reviewer.

## Clarification applied

In the pulmonary barotrauma card, make drainage of a confirmed pneumothorax explicit rather than “when required”:

> Treat tension pneumothorax immediately; a confirmed pneumothorax requires specialist-coordinated pleural drainage before recompression.

Maintain selective chest imaging for suspected pneumothorax, rather than a routine imaging barrier for every patient.

## Findings

- Neurological DCI and suspected AGE appropriately trigger immediate oxygen and urgent specialist-directed recompression. Routine MRI is not a prerequisite. The proposed spinal-pathway exception preserves urgent investigation for a genuine competing compressive or traumatic emergency.
- S2k guidance supports imaging when pneumothorax is suspected, while discouraging routine imaging delays. It supports early HBOT even when presentation is delayed.
- Oxygen, serial neurological documentation, perfusion-guided isotonic fluids, and coordinated transport fit specialist guidance. Avoiding head-down positioning is appropriate.
- The mild-disease lane correctly requires diving-medicine assessment rather than allowing an ED self-discharge rule. The 2018 consensus permits selected specialist-managed mild cases without recompression, while S2k uses a narrower mild category. The outline does not incorrectly merge these definitions.
- Transport advice appropriately avoids a universal flight-altitude or no-fly cutoff and leaves acuity, cabin pressure, route, and destination to retrieval/diving specialists.
- The stop panel limits the steroid/antithrombotic caution to empiric treatment solely for DCI. It does not prohibit a separately indicated treatment. Preserve that distinction in implementation, including ordinary inpatient thromboprophylaxis when indicated.
- No numerical medication regimen or chamber table has been proposed. No new image requires review.
