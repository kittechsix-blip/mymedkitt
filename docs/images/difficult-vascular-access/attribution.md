These original illustrations are not Teleflex artwork and do not imply manufacturer endorsement. Anatomical proportions, target-region circles and the humeral direction inset are schematic, not measured puncture points or insertion-depth guides. No numeric site offsets are depicted. Distal femur anatomy is pediatric; patient age, growth plates, device authorization and local policy must be checked. Use palpation, patient-specific landmarks, the accompanying sourced clinical instructions and the current device IFU. The humeral hand-over-abdomen instruction denotes internal shoulder rotation; it is not a thumb-position instruction.
