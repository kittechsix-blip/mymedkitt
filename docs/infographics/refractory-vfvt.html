Refractory VF/VT: DOSE-VF Escalation Protocol

Adults ≥18 with VF or pulseless VT persisting after 3+ defibrillation attempts + standard ACLS (epinephrine + antiarrhythmic) in the ED, prehospital, or ICU resuscitation setting. Excludes torsades de pointes (separate consult), PEA/asystole, and pediatric arrest.

⚠ Hyperkalemia suspected: give calcium chloride 1 g IV (or calcium gluconate 3 g IV) FIRST for membrane stabilization — calcium does not lower serum K+; follow with regular insulin 10 units + D50 50 mL IV and albuterol 10-20 mg nebulized to shift K+ intracellularly, and NaHCO3 50-100 mEq IV if acidotic. Continue ACLS throughout.
⚠ Toxin-induced VF/VT: match the antidote to the toxin — NaHCO3 1-2 mEq/kg IV (target pH 7.5) for TCA; glucagon 5-10 mg IV + high-dose insulin for beta-blocker; calcium chloride 1-3 g IV + high-dose insulin for CCB; DigiFab for digoxin; Intralipid 20% 1.5 mL/kg bolus for local anesthetic systemic toxicity (LAST); benzodiazepines (avoid beta-blockers) for cocaine. Continue ACLS while administering antidotes.
⚠ Massive PE with arrest: give alteplase 50 mg IV bolus (half the systemic dose) and continue CPR 60-90 minutes after the thrombolytic — relative contraindications to lysis may be overridden in the arrest setting because PE is a reversible cause.
⚠ Do NOT give high-dose epinephrine routinely in cardiac arrest (Class 3: No Benefit, 2025 AHA) — standard dose is 1 mg IV/IO push every 3-5 minutes.
⚠ Perform vector change (AP pad position) BEFORE adding a second defibrillator for DSD — it is faster and may be sufficient alone (DOSE-VF: 21.7% survival vs 13.3% standard).
⚠ ECPR contraindications: known severe neurologic impairment pre-arrest, metastatic malignancy or terminal illness, no-flow >5 minutes (no bystander CPR), prolonged low-flow >60 minutes in a non-hypothermic patient, irreversible primary disease, or severe sepsis/hemorrhagic shock.

1 · Recognition & Optimize Basics

Refractory VF/pVT: VF or pulseless VT persisting after 3+ defibrillation attempts with 2-minute CPR cycles PLUS standard ACLS (epinephrine + antiarrhythmic). 2025 AHA prefers the term "Persisting VF/pVT." Subtypes: true shock-refractory (VF never terminates), recurrent VF (terminates but refibrillates <5 sec), electrical storm (3+ sustained VT/VF in 24h). Incidence 10-25% of OHCA; survival only 2-12% with standard care.

  1. Pad placement: standard is anterolateral (AL) — right infraclavicular + left lateral chest
  2. After 3 failed shocks → switch to anterior-posterior (AP) vector — left parasternal + left infrascapular
  3. CPR rate 100-120/min, depth ≥2 inches (5 cm), full chest recoil
  4. Minimize interruptions to <10 sec for shocks
  5. Consider mechanical CPR (LUCAS, AutoPulse) for prolonged resuscitation
