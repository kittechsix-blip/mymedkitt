Cluster Headache: Recognize, Abort, Bridge, Prevent

Adults presenting to the ED with suspected or known cluster headache (ICHD-3 §3.1). Excludes pediatrics. Any red-flag-positive or atypical presentation must complete secondary workup before being treated on this pathway (see scopeWarnings).

⚠ Cluster phenotype overlaps SAH, cervical artery dissection, CVST, AACG, hypertensive emergency/pre-eclampsia, and meningitis — exclude all SNOOP10 red flags before treating any patient as primary cluster headache.
⚠ First-line acute therapy is 100% O2 12-15 L/min via NON-REBREATHER mask for 15 minutes, seated and leaning forward — nasal cannula (peak FiO2 ~40%) is inadequate and must not be substituted.
⚠ Triptans (sumatriptan, zolmitriptan) are contraindicated in CAD/ischemic heart disease, uncontrolled hypertension, stroke or MI within 6 months, hemiplegic/basilar migraine, and pregnancy. Never give a triptan within 24 hours of an ergot derivative (DHE) — additive vasoconstriction.
⚠ SQ sumatriptan is capped at 2 injections (12 mg) per 24 hours with at least 1 hour between doses.
⚠ Verapamil for cluster requires a baseline 12-lead ECG before the first dose, a repeat ECG before EVERY dose escalation, and another ECG about 10 days after each escalation. HALT titration for PR interval >0.22 s, any new or worsening AV block, bradycardia <50 bpm, or symptomatic hypotension.
⚠ Lithium (chronic cluster only) has a narrow therapeutic window — toxicity begins above 1.2 mEq/L. Avoid or dose-adjust with thiazides, ACE-inhibitors/ARBs, NSAIDs (including indomethacin), metronidazole, and any cause of dehydration.
⚠ Cluster headache carries the highest suicidal-ideation rate of any primary headache disorder ("suicide headache") — screen for SI at every encounter and provide crisis resources (988) before discharge.

5 · Oxygen — first-line acute Rx

  1. Seat patient upright, leaning forward
  2. Apply 100% O2 via non-rebreather mask at 12-15 L/min (NOT nasal cannula — peak FiO2 ~40% is inadequate)
  3. Reassess at 7 minutes — if no response, add SQ sumatriptan without waiting the full 15 min
  4. Reassess at 15 minutes for full abortion
  5. If aborted: arrange home O2 E-cylinder + NRB standby plan and proceed to bridge therapy
Onset ~5-10 min, full abort by 15 min in 78% (Cohen JAMA 2009 RCT; AAN/AHS 2016). Demand-valve mask may be more effective than NRB if available. No meaningful contraindication in the ED setting.

10 · Patient education + safety

Avoid during a bout
Alcohol (any amount — even outside a bout triptans/O2 remain the plan, but during a bout alcohol reliably triggers an attack), nitroglycerin/nitrates, high altitude/hypoxic environments, strong solvent/paint fumes.
  1. Home O2 E-cylinder + NRB mask (DME order)
  2. SQ sumatriptan 6 mg autoinjectors (max 2 doses/24h, ≥1 h apart)
  3. IN zolmitriptan 5 mg as SQ backup
  4. Bridge medication (prednisone taper OR documented ONB plan)
  5. Maintenance prescription (verapamil with explicit titration + ECG follow-up plan)
Safety screen — highest SI of any primary headache disorder
Cluster has been called "suicide headache" for a reason. Screen for suicidal ideation, provide 988, discuss safety planning, and ensure follow-up within 1 week.

11 · Disposition

Discharge if
Attack aborted in ED, bridge therapy initiated, maintenance verapamil started with written titration + ECG schedule, acute rescue prescriptions given, home O2 ordered, neurology follow-up within 1 week, safety screen completed.
Admit if
Attack does not abort despite O2 + 2 SQ triptans + ONB + IN lidocaine; active suicidal ideation; cannot arrange outpatient O2 before the next anticipated attack; initiating lithium without reliable monitoring; concurrent verapamil bradycardia/hypotension.
Other pitfalls4 items
Do NOT use nasal cannula for the O2 trial — peak FiO2 ~40% is inadequate for cluster.
Do NOT give DHE within 24 hours of any triptan — additive vasoconstriction.
Do NOT delay bridge/maintenance therapy — verapamil takes 2-4 weeks to titrate, so the bridge must start the same day.
Do NOT escalate verapamil without a repeat ECG at every step — PR prolongation (19%) and AV block (4%) occur across the titration range.

Cluster has been called "suicide headache" for a reason. Screen for suicidal ideation, provide 988, discuss safety planning, and ensure follow-up within 1 week.

11 · Disposition

Attack aborted in ED, bridge therapy initiated, maintenance verapamil started with written titration + ECG schedule, acute rescue prescriptions given, home O2 ordered, neurology follow-up within 1 week, safety screen completed.

Attack does not abort despite O2 + 2 SQ triptans + ONB + IN lidocaine; active suicidal ideation; cannot arrange outpatient O2 before the next anticipated attack; initiating lithium without reliable monitoring; concurrent verapamil bradycardia/hypotension.

Other pitfalls

**Do NOT** use nasal cannula for the O2 trial — peak FiO2 ~40% is inadequate for cluster.

**Do NOT** give DHE within 24 hours of any triptan — additive vasoconstriction.

**Do NOT** delay bridge/maintenance therapy — verapamil takes 2-4 weeks to titrate, so the bridge must start the same day.

**Do NOT** escalate verapamil without a repeat ECG at every step — PR prolongation (19%) and AV block (4%) occur across the titration range.
