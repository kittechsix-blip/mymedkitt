Kratom Withdrawal: Assess, Treat, Dispo

Adults with a history of kratom (Mitragyna speciosa) use presenting with an opioid-like withdrawal syndrome in the ED. COWS is not formally validated for kratom but is used as a severity proxy. Excludes pediatrics and withdrawal from full opioid agonists without a kratom-use history.

⚠ Cardiac complications are documented at withdrawal peak (day 3-4) — bradycardia progressing to cardiac arrest has been reported. Obtain an ECG and consider continuous monitoring in moderate-severe presentations.
⚠ Avoid naltrexone during active kratom use or withdrawal — it can precipitate kratom withdrawal similar to opioid withdrawal. Taper kratom first if planning to start naltrexone for OUD.
⚠ Do NOT start buprenorphine before COWS ≥8 — starting too early can precipitate withdrawal.
⚠ ICU-level care (continuous cardiac monitoring ± dexmedetomidine) is indicated for HR <50 with symptoms, arrhythmia on ECG, hemodynamic instability, altered mental status, or seizures.
⚠ Ask about product type — high-potency 7-OH extracts, shots, and tinctures cause 5-10x more severe withdrawal than traditional leaf/powder.

1 · Recognition & timeline

Kratom (Mitragyna speciosa) contains alkaloids that act as partial μ-opioid agonists. Chronic use causes dependence with opioid-like withdrawal on cessation — milder intensity but LONGER duration (1-3 weeks vs 5-7 days for opioids), with peak symptoms on day 3-4 rather than day 1-2. Ask about product type: traditional leaf/powder is lower-risk; extracts, shots, and tinctures with 7-OH are 5-10x more potent and cause worse withdrawal.

Onset6-12 hours~12-24 hours
PeakDays 3-424-36 hours
Acute phase4-7 days5-7 days
PAWS / full resolutionUp to 3 months~1 week
CategoryFindings
Opioid-like (78% report)Fatigue, insomnia, anxiety, myalgias, lacrimation, rhinorrhea, diaphoresis, mydriasis, GI upset, tachycardia
Unique to kratomTremors, jerky movements, coordination deficits, protracted depression/brain fog, severe fatigue (86%), rare seizures, cardiac complications

2 · Assessment

COWS is not formally validated for kratom but is a reasonable severity proxy — it may miss kratom-unique features (tremor, coordination, brain fog). Supplement with vitals, neuro exam, timeline/dose, product type, and prior opioid-use history. Enter the COWS score above to drive the recommendations below.

8 · Disposition

Discharge — mild
COWS <13, stable vitals, tolerating PO, no cardiac/neuro concerns. Discharge with clonidine 0.1 mg q6h PRN, gabapentin 300 mg TID (if prescribed), loperamide/ondansetron/NSAID-APAP PRN x 5-7 days. Warn: peak is day 3-4, symptoms may worsen before improving. Return for chest pain, palpitations, severe vomiting, confusion, or seizure. Follow-up with PCP or addiction medicine within 1 week.
ED observation — moderate
COWS 13-24: 6-12 hour observation, reassess COWS q2-4h, titrate buprenorphine or clonidine, IV fluids if dehydrated, ECG for QTc. Discharge when COWS <13 and stable 2+ hours, tolerating PO, stable vitals. Escalate to admission if worsening or complications emerge.
Admit — severe/complications
COWS >24 with instability, cardiac concerns, seizure risk, severe agitation needing IV medication, intractable vomiting/dehydration, altered mental status, inability to tolerate PO, or inadequate social support. Continuous cardiac monitoring, treatment pathway, IV fluids/electrolytes, dexmedetomidine if severe agitation, serial COWS q4-6h, ECG on admission. Addiction medicine and toxicology consults as needed. Expected stay 24-72 hours (longer if ICU-level care needed).
