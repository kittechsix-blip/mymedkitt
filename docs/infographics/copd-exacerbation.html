AECOPD: Target SpO2 88-92%, Escalate Early

Adults ≥18 in the ED with an acute exacerbation of COPD (AECOPD). Excludes pediatrics, primary asthma without COPD overlap, and non-COPD causes of dyspnea (CHF, PE, pneumothorax) — each needs a different pathway.

⚠ Target SpO2 88-92% — NOT higher. Hyperoxia in COPD worsens V/Q mismatch and CO2 retention; do not chase a normal saturation.
⚠ Do NOT delay BiPAP to wait for an ABG in severe AECOPD — patients who look terrible often improve rapidly once BiPAP is started.
⚠ Permissive hypercapnia is required post-intubation — target pH 7.25-7.35, not a normal CO2. Raising the rate to chase CO2 causes breath-stacking and hypotension.
⚠ Ketamine is the preferred induction agent (bronchodilation, BP preserved); propofol causes hypotension — have a vasopressor ready regardless of which agent is used.
⚠ Avoid benzodiazepines and opioids for BiPAP sedation — both blunt respiratory drive; use dexmedetomidine if sedation is needed for mask tolerance.

ICU Admission
Continue BiPAP or mechanical ventilation, scheduled bronchodilators, and steroids (methylprednisolone 125 mg IV daily x 1-2 days, then PO). Orders: continuous SpO2/cardiac monitoring, ABG/VBG q4-6h until stable, respiratory therapy q4-6h. Goal: wean respiratory support, identify and treat the precipitant.[1][2]
Telemetry/Floor Admission

ED Discharge
Mild exacerbation, returned to baseline, SpO2 >90% RA, reliable follow-up. Prednisone 40 mg PO daily x 5 days, antibiotics if criteria met. 8 · Do NOT

Critical pitfalls

**Do NOT target SpO2 >92% in AECOPD** — hyperoxia worsens hypercapnia via V/Q mismatch and loss of hypoxic drive.

**Do NOT delay BiPAP initiation to obtain an ABG** in severe AECOPD.

**Do NOT sedate a BiPAP patient with benzodiazepines or opioids** — use dexmedetomidine (no respiratory depression).

**Do NOT chase a normal CO2 on the ventilator post-intubation** — accept permissive hypercapnia (pH 7.25-7.35).

**Do NOT continue continuous BiPAP for more than 48 hours** without a wean trial — risk of nasal ulceration; step down to HFNC/NC as tolerated.

**Do NOT give antibiotics for every AECOPD** — reserve for purulent sputum + 1 other cardinal symptom, or NIV/ICU requirement; narrow-spectrum agents suffice.
