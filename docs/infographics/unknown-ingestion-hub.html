Unknown Ingestion Hub — Toxidrome-Driven ED Workup

ED evaluation of a known or suspected unknown ingestion/poisoning where the substance, dose, or timeline is uncertain. Structured around 12 time-critical toxidrome patterns plus an undifferentiated physiology-sorting pathway, driving toward resuscitation, decontamination, and antidote therapy in that order.

⚠ Send glucose, ECG, ABG/VBG, acetaminophen level, and salicylate level on EVERY unknown ingestion — do not skip APAP + salicylate even if the patient denies them.
⚠ Treat the toxidrome you see, not the substance the patient claims.
⚠ Order: resuscitation → decontamination → antidote. Never invert — activated charcoal without an airway plan is aspiration pneumonia.
⚠ AVOID intubation in salicylate toxicity unless absolutely necessary — apnea plus acidosis is catastrophic; the patient is breathing off the acid.
⚠ Physostigmine is CONTRAINDICATED with any QRS prolongation, known/suspected TCA, or unknown co-ingestion.
⚠ Xylazine sedation does NOT reverse with naloxone — titrate naloxone to respirations, not consciousness, to avoid precipitated withdrawal and pulmonary edema.

Opioid / xylazine toxidrome

Resus / decon / antidote — in that order

  1. Airway — RSI with ketamine/etomidate; succinylcholine OK except organophosphate/burn/hyperK.
  2. Breathing — ventilate to match metabolic acidosis; salicylate and toxic alcohol patients need high minute ventilation.
  3. Circulation — fluids, then norepinephrine; calcium + HDI for CCB/BB; bicarb for Na-channel; lipid emulsion as rescue for severe lipophilic tox.
Activated Charcoal
1 g/kg PO/NG slurry
PO/NG
ONLY if airway protected, ingestion <1–2h, agent binds charcoal, and not contraindicated (caustic, hydrocarbon, metals, alcohols).
  • Whole-bowel irrigation — body packers, sustained-release CCB, iron, lithium; only if airway protected.
  • Gastric lavage — essentially abandoned; consider only for life-threatening, very-recent, non-bound ingestion with airway protection.
  • Skin/eye decon — organophosphate, caustic, hydrofluoric acid.
Antidote third
See toxidrome-specific dosing above. Lipid emulsion 1.5 mL/kg bolus + infusion as rescue for lipophilic cardiotoxin arrest.

DO NOT: force emesis, neutralize caustics, dose charcoal without an airway plan.

Reassess after initial bundle

Toxicology trajectories diverge fast — resolving sympathomimetics look like ventricular ectopy on the way down, salicylate looks great until the brain swells. Reassess physiology q15–30 min in any ingestion.

TrajectoryNext step
Worse: rising QRS, falling pH, AMS, hemodynamic collapseReturn to exclusions and escalate antidote/dialysis
Stable, diagnosis unclear, levels pendingRefine with imaging + labs
Improving with clear toxidrome and antidote effectMove to disposition checklist

Imaging + labs

Trust the mandatory bundle already sent at sick-check (glucose, electrolytes, BUN/Cr, lactate, AG, osmolar gap, APAP, salicylate, ABG/VBG, ECG). Add levels/imaging only when they will change the next 15 minutes of care.

Add-onWhen it changes management
Urine drug screenLOW yield, slow, false positives/negatives — do not let it change management; skip unless required for psych/disposition

CXRAspiration, ARDS (salicylate, sympathomimetic), pulmonary edema (opioid, naloxone-precipitated)

Disposition

Disposition follows trajectory + agent half-life + intentional vs. unintentional ingestion.

TrajectoryDisposition
Ongoing instability, antidote infusion, refractory acidosis, intubationICU / resus admission
Stable but ongoing observation needed (sustained-release, long half-life, psych hold)Admit / observe
Asymptomatic past observation window, medically clear, intentional ingestionMedical clearance + psych disposition
Asymptomatic past observation window, accidental, normal labs/ECGDischarge with return precautions

ICU / resus admission

Loop in toxicology
ICU for: intubation, vasopressor/inotrope requirement, dialysis, antidote infusion (fomepizole, NAC infusion, HDI, lipid emulsion), refractory acidosis, persistent QRS prolongation, ongoing seizures, hyperthermia, body packer with leak. Call regional Poison Control + medical toxicology for ALL ICU-level ingestions.

Admit / observation

When in doubt, observe one more half-life
Admit for: sustained-release agent (CCB, lithium, bupropion, salicylate), long half-life (digoxin, methadone, phenobarbital), staggered APAP, ongoing supportive care, persistent mild toxicity, social inability to manage. Observation: 6h for most short-acting agents with normal repeat ECG/exam; 24h+ for sustained-release.

Medical clearance + psych disposition

Do not psych-clear until APAP/salicylate trajectory is established
Clearance criteria: vitals normal, exam normal, repeat ECG normal, mandatory labs back and reassuring, observation window appropriate for the agent passed. Document agent(s), dose, time, intent, and clearance criteria met.

Pitfall: APAP can be normal at 4h and lethal at 24h with staggered ingestion — recheck level + LFTs at 24h if any doubt about timing.

Discharge checklist

Document the observation window and precautions
Discharge only when: asymptomatic through the full observation window for the agent, normal repeat vitals/ECG, mandatory labs normal, accidental/exploratory ingestion in a competent adult or supervised child, reliable caregiver and follow-up.

Return precautions: any new symptom, vomiting, drowsiness, behavior change, jaundice, dark urine, decreased urine output, palpitations, syncope. Call Poison Control 1-800-222-1222 for any concern.

  • Gastric lavage — essentially abandoned; consider only for life-threatening, very-recent, non-bound ingestion with airway protection.
  • Skin/eye decon — organophosphate, caustic, hydrofluoric acid.

See toxidrome-specific dosing above. Lipid emulsion 1.5 mL/kg bolus + infusion as rescue for lipophilic cardiotoxin arrest.

DO NOT: force emesis, neutralize caustics, dose charcoal without an airway plan.

Reassess after initial bundle

Toxicology trajectories diverge fast — resolving sympathomimetics look like ventricular ectopy on the way down, salicylate looks great until the brain swells. Reassess physiology q15–30 min in any ingestion.

Trajectory | Next step

Worse: rising QRS, falling pH, AMS, hemodynamic collapse | Return to exclusions and escalate antidote/dialysis

Stable, diagnosis unclear, levels pending | Refine with imaging + labs

Improving with clear toxidrome and antidote effect | Move to disposition checklist

Imaging + labs

Trust the mandatory bundle already sent at sick-check (glucose, electrolytes, BUN/Cr, lactate, AG, osmolar gap, APAP, salicylate, ABG/VBG, ECG). Add levels/imaging only when they will change the next 15 minutes of care.

Add-on | When it changes management

Urine drug screen | LOW yield, slow, false positives/negatives — do not let it change management; skip unless required for psych/disposition

Specific levels | Lithium, digoxin, valproate, phenytoin, carbamazepine, iron, theophylline — any sustained-release agent worth a level

CXR | Aspiration, ARDS (salicylate, sympathomimetic), pulmonary edema (opioid, naloxone-precipitated)

Abdominal XR | Radiopaque tablets (CHIPES); body packer/stuffer

CT head | AMS without explanation, focal neuro deficit, head trauma concern, suspected anticoagulant hemorrhage

CT abdomen | Suspected perforation (caustic), bowel ischemia, body packer with concerning physiology

Echo / cardiac POCUS | Severe overdose with hypotension — cardiogenic (CCB, beta-blocker, TCA) vs. distributive (sympathomimetic post-burnout) shock

Disposition

Disposition follows trajectory + agent half-life + intentional vs. unintentional ingestion.

Trajectory | Disposition

Ongoing instability, antidote infusion, refractory acidosis, intubation | ICU / resus admission

Stable but ongoing observation needed (sustained-release, long half-life, psych hold) | Admit / observe

Asymptomatic past observation window, medically clear, intentional ingestion | Medical clearance + psych disposition

Asymptomatic past observation window, accidental, normal labs/ECG | Discharge with return precautions

ICU / resus admission

ICU for: intubation, vasopressor/inotrope requirement, dialysis, antidote infusion (fomepizole, NAC infusion, HDI, lipid emulsion), refractory acidosis, persistent QRS prolongation, ongoing seizures, hyperthermia, body packer with leak. Call regional Poison Control + medical toxicology for ALL ICU-level ingestions.

Admit / observation

Admit for: sustained-release agent (CCB, lithium, bupropion, salicylate), long half-life (digoxin, methadone, phenobarbital), staggered APAP, ongoing supportive care, persistent mild toxicity, social inability to manage. Observation: 6h for most short-acting agents with normal repeat ECG/exam; 24h+ for sustained-release.

Medical clearance + psych disposition

Clearance criteria: vitals normal, exam normal, repeat ECG normal, mandatory labs back and reassuring, observation window appropriate for the agent passed. Document agent(s), dose, time, intent, and clearance criteria met.

Pitfall: APAP can be normal at 4h and lethal at 24h with staggered ingestion — recheck level + LFTs at 24h if any doubt about timing.

Discharge checklist

Discharge only when: asymptomatic through the full observation window for the agent, normal repeat vitals/ECG, mandatory labs normal, accidental/exploratory ingestion in a competent adult or supervised child, reliable caregiver and follow-up.

Return precautions: any new symptom, vomiting, drowsiness, behavior change, jaundice, dark urine, decreased urine output, palpitations, syncope. Call Poison Control 1-800-222-1222 for any concern.

Full algorithm

Unknown Ingestion — Sick Check First — Bedside glucose, naloxone trial, ECG, ABG, and the universal lab bundle — APAP + salicylate always.

Time-Critical Exclusions — match the toxidrome — 12 toxidrome buckets cover the high-mortality ingestions; default to the undifferentiated pathway when none fit.

TCA / Na-channel blocker → bicarb — Wide QRS is the trigger — bicarb goes in before the level returns.

Salicylate → bicarb + dialysis — Bicarbonate + dialysis. Intubation is dangerous — the patient breathes off the acid.

Acetaminophen → NAC + nomogram — When in doubt, start NAC. Risk of treatment is negligible vs cost of missed hepatotoxicity.

Opioid / xylazine → titrate naloxone — Titrate naloxone to breathing. Xylazine sedation will not reverse with naloxone.

Sympathomimetic → benzos + cooling — Benzos first. Cooling second. Beta-blockers last and never alone.

Cholinergic / OP → atropine + pralidoxime — Atropine endpoint is dry secretions, not heart rate. Decontaminate the patient AND the staff.

No clean toxidrome — sort by physiology — Drive workup with derangements (QRS, AG, osm gap, lactate, glucose, temperature). Poison Control 1-800-222-1222.

Anticholinergic → benzos + cooling — Benzos and cooling for everyone. Physostigmine only when QRS is narrow and the toxidrome is pure.

Serotonin syndrome → stop agents + cool — Clonus + recent serotonergic agent is the recognition combo. Cooling beats meds.

CO ± cyanide → O2 + hydroxocobalamin — Treat CO and cyanide simultaneously in fire victims. Do not wait for cyanide level.

Caustic → airway, NPO, endoscopy — Airway first. Endoscopy is for staging, not therapy.

Toxic alcohols → fomepizole — Empiric fomepizole pays for itself in saved retinas and kidneys.

Digoxin → Digibind — Hyperkalemia + brady + GI + visual = call Digibind in. Calcium is debated.

Resus → decon → antidote (in that order) — Stabilize physiology before decontamination. Charcoal is narrow. Lipid emulsion is the cardiac-arrest rescue.

Reassess after initial bundle — Reassess physiology q15-30 min; deterioration triggers a return to exclusions.

Imaging + labs — Trust the mandatory bundle. Add levels/imaging only when they will change the next 15 minutes of care.

Disposition — ICU for instability/infusion; admit if half-life or sustained-release demands it; psych path for intentional; discharge only after clear observation.

ICU / resus admission — Loop in toxicology. Specialty dosing and antidote logistics save lives in the second 24 hours.

Admit / observe — When in doubt, observe one more half-life. Tox rebounds are real.

Medical clearance + psych — Do not psych-clear until APAP/salicylate trajectory is established.

Discharge + return precautions — Document the observation window, agent half-life, and the precautions you gave.
