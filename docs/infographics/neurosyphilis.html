Neurosyphilis: Evaluation & Treatment

Adults with confirmed syphilis (any stage) being evaluated for neurologic, ocular, or otic involvement, in the ED/inpatient setting. Excludes pediatric/congenital syphilis and pregnancy-specific dosing.

⚠ CSF-VDRL reactive is diagnostic of neurosyphilis — start IV penicillin G immediately.
⚠ Aqueous crystalline penicillin G 18–24 million units/day IV × 10–14 days is first-line treatment.
⚠ CSF pleocytosis (>5 WBC/µL) or elevated protein with neuro symptoms → treat as neurosyphilis even if CSF-VDRL is negative.
⚠ Ocular or otic syphilis: treat same as neurosyphilis — ophthalmology/ENT consult mandatory.
⚠ Repeat CSF at 6 months — CSF pleocytosis should normalize first (protein/VDRL lag).
⚠ PCN allergy: desensitization preferred over ceftriaxone alternative (limited data for ceftriaxone).
⚠ Do NOT use benzathine penicillin for neurosyphilis — does NOT penetrate the blood-brain barrier.
⚠ Ocular/otic syphilis: CSF examination is recommended but treatment should NOT be delayed for LP.

Neurosyphilis — Suspect & Screen

Neurosyphilis should be considered in any patient with confirmed syphilis who has neurological symptoms or signs. Diagnosis requires a combination of clinical, serologic, and CSF findings.

Key presentations: visual changes, hearing loss, cognitive impairment, psychiatric symptoms, cranial nerve deficits, meningismus, stroke-like symptoms, tabes dorsalis (late), general paresis (late).

What To Do Now

Order CSF evaluation
Neurological symptoms in a patient with syphilis → CSF-VDRL, FTA-ABS, cell count, protein, glucose.
Confirmed neurosyphilis — treat immediately
CSF-VDRL reactive is diagnostic. Start Penicillin G 18–24 million units/day IV × 10–14 days now. Admit; ID consult.
Probable neurosyphilis — treat as confirmed
CSF-VDRL non-reactive but pleocytosis >5 WBC/µL or elevated protein with neuro symptoms → treat as neurosyphilis. Same regimen as confirmed.
Neurosyphilis unlikely — treat underlying stage
Normal CSF with non-reactive VDRL. Treat the syphilis stage instead (early vs. late latent) and consider alternative diagnoses for the neuro symptoms.
Ocular/otic syphilis — treat now, do not wait for LP
Treat as neurosyphilis regardless of CSF findings and regardless of stage. Ophthalmology and/or ENT consult mandatory; ID consult. CSF exam recommended but must NOT delay treatment.
No CSF indicated — treat late latent syphilis
Routine LP is NOT recommended for asymptomatic late latent syphilis without exception criteria. Treat with benzathine penicillin per stage.
Exception criteria present — CSF exam indicated
Treatment failure, HIV co-infection, developing neuro signs, or tertiary syphilis → order CSF evaluation as above.
