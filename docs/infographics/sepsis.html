Sepsis & Septic Shock: The First Golden Hour

Adults ≥18 with suspected sepsis or septic shock in the ED or ICU. Excludes pediatrics, pregnancy-specific protocols, and neutropenic-fever pathways — each needs modified management.

⚠ Antibiotics within 1 hour of recognition — each hour of delay increases mortality. Give the beta-lactam FIRST when also giving vancomycin (improved survival).
⚠ Optimize MAP before intubation — peri-intubation hemodynamic collapse is common in sepsis. Use push-dose phenylephrine or start norepinephrine first; ketamine for induction.
⚠ Norepinephrine is first-line — start 0.05 mcg/kg/min, target MAP ≥65 mmHg. NOT dopamine (more arrhythmias and higher mortality, SOAP-II).
⚠ 30 mL/kg balanced crystalloid (Lactated Ringer's) within 3 hours in 500 mL boluses, reassessing between each. Avoid HES (increased mortality). RESTRICT further fluids after initial resuscitation.
⚠ Add vasopressin 0.03–0.04 units/min EARLY before escalating norepinephrine to high doses, and start hydrocortisone 50 mg IV q6h for vasopressor-dependent shock.

1 · Recognize & classify

Sepsis-3 definition
Sepsis = life-threatening organ dysfunction from a dysregulated host response to infection — clinically, suspected infection + SOFA increase ≥2.[1] Septic shock = vasopressor requirement to keep MAP ≥65 mmHg AND lactate >2 mmol/L despite adequate fluids. Mortality ≥40%.[1][3]
The one principle
Early recognition → aggressive resuscitation → source control → antibiotics within 1 hour. Duration of hypotension before effective antibiotics is the single strongest predictor of survival.[2][20]

2 · Hour-1 bundle

  1. Draw 2 sets of blood cultures from 2 separate peripheral sites BEFORE antibiotics — do not draw through indwelling lines.[2][24]
  2. Send serum lactate, CBC, BMP, LFTs, coagulation studies, VBG within 45 minutes — do NOT let labs delay fluids or antibiotics.[24]
  3. Start 30 mL/kg balanced crystalloid (Lactated Ringer's preferred) in 500 mL boluses, reassessing between each.[2][24]
  4. Give the beta-lactam backbone within 1 hour of recognition — before vancomycin if both are indicated.[2][24]
Fluid choice & limits
Balanced crystalloid beats saline (SMART) and avoid HES — the 6S trial showed increased mortality (51 vs 43%).[10] After the initial 30 mL/kg, RESTRICT further fluids; most crystalloid extravasates and ongoing boluses are often harmful (CLASSIC).[9]

3 · Antibiotics & MRSA coverage

Beta-lactam backbone (choose one): Piperacillin-Tazobactam 4.5 g IV q6h (extended 4-h infusion), Cefepime 2 g IV q8h, or Meropenem 1 g IV q8h (reserve for MDR risk or severe PCN allergy).[23][24]

Sequence matters
Give vancomycin AFTER the beta-lactam — beta-lactam first improves survival. Load 25–30 mg/kg, then 15–20 mg/kg q8–12h. Discontinue vancomycin if no MRSA is cultured within 48 hours.[24]
—Daptomycin (6 mg/kg) — if vancomycin contraindicated
Daptomycin does NOT cover pneumonia (inactivated by surfactant) — use Linezolid 600 mg IV q12h instead when the lung is the source.[24]

4 · Hemodynamics & shock

Flip Septic shock above once the patient needs a vasopressor to hold MAP ≥65 despite 30 mL/kg. Flip Refractory if shock persists on norepinephrine + vasopressin.

First-line: norepinephrine
Norepinephrine — start 0.05 mcg/kg/min (4–6 mcg/min), titrate by 0.02–0.05 mcg/kg/min to MAP ≥65 mmHg. May run peripherally for <6 h while obtaining central access.[2][24] Not dopamine — SOAP-II showed more arrhythmias and higher mortality.[11]
Add EARLY, not late
Add Vasopressin 0.03–0.04 units/min (fixed, non-titratable) before escalating norepinephrine to high doses — NE-sparing, lower AF risk.[12][13] Start Hydrocortisone 50 mg IV q6h for vasopressor-dependent shock — shortens shock duration (ADRENAL, APROCCHSS).[14][15]
Refractory shock — reassess first
Before more drugs, hunt the cause: missed/undrained source, wrong diagnosis (adrenal crisis, thyroid storm, PE), inadequate antibiotics, or septic cardiomyopathy.[23] Then Methylene Blue as salvage — bolus then infusion (Ibarra-Estrada 2023 RCT: shorter time to vasopressor discontinuation).[21]
—Methylene blue bolus — low (1 mg/kg)
—Methylene blue bolus — high (2 mg/kg)
—Methylene blue infusion (0.5 mg/kg/h, over 1 h)
Resuscitation endpoints & MAP targetstargets

Follow continuously: MAP ≥65 mmHg, urine output ≥0.5 mL/kg/hr, capillary refill time <3 s (ANDROMEDA-SHOCK), mentation improving.[2][16][24]

Personalize the target: chronic hypertension → MAP 80–85 (SEPSISPAM, better renal outcomes but more AF); previously normotensive/younger → 60–65 may suffice (65-Trial).[18][19]

5 · What to do NOW

Refractory septic shock
Reassess for a missed source, wrong diagnosis, and antibiotic gaps. Continue norepinephrine + vasopressin + hydrocortisone; add methylene blue salvage (doses in section 4). Add dobutamine 2–5 mcg/kg/min if POCUS shows septic cardiomyopathy. Disposition: ICU.
Septic shock — pressor-dependent
Norepinephrine 0.05 mcg/kg/min to MAP ≥65; add vasopressin 0.03–0.04 units/min early and hydrocortisone 50 mg IV q6h. Confirm the beta-lactam is in and source control is arranged. Disposition: ICU.
Sepsis without shock — responding
MAP ≥65 off pressors after fluids + antibiotics. RESTRICT further fluids, de-escalate antibiotics by culture at 48–72 h, and transfuse only for Hgb ≤7 g/dL (TRISS).[22] Disposition: ward or step-down with close monitoring.
