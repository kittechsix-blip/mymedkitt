Dental Avulsion: Time-Critical Reimplantation

ED management of a completely avulsed (fully displaced) tooth, adults and children. Assumes permanent or uncertain dentition until confirmed; primary (baby) teeth are identified and excluded from reimplantation at the first branch. Excludes non-avulsion dental trauma (fracture, luxation without full displacement) and general maxillofacial trauma resuscitation, which follow separate protocols.

⚠ TIME IS TOOTH — reimplantation success drops with extraoral dry time: <5 min ~95%, 15 min ~80%, 30 min ~50%, >60 min dry = poor prognosis.
⚠ NEVER reimplant primary (baby) teeth — risk of damaging the underlying permanent tooth bud.
⚠ Handle the avulsed tooth by the CROWN only — never touch, scrub, or scrape the root; never wrap it in dry gauze/tissue.
⚠ Best storage: HBSS (up to 24h) or cold milk (up to 6h) — NOT water (hypotonic, kills periodontal ligament cells).
⚠ Reimplant in the ED if a dentist is not available within 15-30 minutes — every minute of extraoral dry time matters.
⚠ If the tooth cannot be located, obtain a CXR (PA and lateral) to rule out aspiration before assuming it was lost or swallowed.
⚠ Use a flexible splint for 2 weeks (4 weeks if dry time was >60 min or an alveolar fracture is present) — rigid splints cause ankylosis.
⚠ Doxycycline 100 mg PO BID × 7 days is first-line antibiotic prophylaxis for adults and children >12y after reimplantation.
⚠ Urgent dental follow-up within 24-48 hours is mandatory after any reimplantation or tooth-storage disposition.
⚠ Do not force reimplantation if there is severe caries, advanced periodontal disease, or an uncooperative patient — store and transport to a dentist instead.

5 · Reimplantation technique & imaging

  1. Prepare the socket: gently irrigate with saline to remove clot; do NOT curette; local anesthesia (lidocaine with epi)
  2. Prepare the tooth: handle by crown only, rinse gently with saline if contaminated, do NOT scrub the root; if dry >60 min, soak 30 min in HBSS or saline
  3. Insert the tooth: orient correctly (curve faces lip), insert with gentle steady pressure, seat fully, patient bites on gauze to hold position
  4. Confirm position: X-ray to confirm seating, check occlusion

3 · Management algorithm

9 · Pitfalls

Common errors to avoid

Do NOT reimplant a primary (baby) tooth — risk of damaging the permanent tooth bud.

Do NOT touch, scrub, or dry the root — handle by the crown only to protect the periodontal ligament.

Do NOT store the tooth in water — it is hypotonic and kills periodontal ligament cells; use HBSS, cold milk, saliva, or saline.

Do NOT use a rigid splint — it causes ankylosis; always splint flexibly.

Do NOT delay CXR when the tooth cannot be located and the patient has any respiratory symptoms — aspiration must be ruled out immediately.

Do NOT keep waiting for a dentist past 15-30 minutes (or 1 hour if already in transit) — reimplant in the ED instead; every minute of dry time costs prognosis.
