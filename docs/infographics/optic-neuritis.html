Optic Neuritis — Confirm, Subtype, Treat, Disposition

Patients presenting with acute optic neuritis (demyelinating optic neuropathy). Confirms the diagnosis by clinical criteria, subtypes into MS-ON, NMOSD, MOG-AD, neuroretinitis, or atypical/expanded workup, and guides MRI protocol, acute IV steroid dosing, and disposition (admit / outpatient infusion / observation).

⚠ MRI brain + orbits with fat-sat + gadolinium before steroids when possible
⚠ IV methylprednisolone 1 g daily × 3-5 days; NEVER oral prednisone alone (ONTT)
⚠ Send AQP4-IgG and MOG-IgG cell-based assays before/with steroid start — in EVERY new optic neuritis
⚠ Neuro-ophthalmology follow-up within 1 week of discharge
⚠ Interferon-beta and natalizumab (MS drugs) WORSEN NMOSD — never start MS DMT without ruling out NMOSD first

Overview

This is the confirm → subtype → treat → dispose spine. See the full protocol for complete atypical-feature detail and the full disposition checklist.

Confirm the diagnosis

Classic ON features: subacute monocular vision loss over hours to days (rarely bilateral simultaneous in adults). Disc exam is two-thirds retrobulbar (normal disc), one-third papillitis (swollen disc) — a normal disc does NOT rule out ON.

  • Pain with eye movement (>90% of typical MS-ON) — the single most useful symptom
  • Color desaturation disproportionate to visual acuity (red looks washed-out, brown, or grey)
  • RAPD on the affected side (may be absent if bilateral and symmetric)
  • Central scotoma on confrontation or formal fields
Atypical features → suspect non-MS etiology
Bilateral simultaneous loss, no light perception, no recovery at 4-6 weeks, severe disc swelling with hemorrhages, macular star, age <12 or >50, or steroid-dependent/non-responsive disease. If any atypical feature: send the full antibody panel and image the spine.

Subtype triage

Subtype drives Rx (IV methylprednisolone alone vs PLEX vs long-term immunosuppression) and prognosis. Send AQP4-IgG and MOG-IgG cell-based assays in EVERY new optic neuritis, before or with steroid start — the assay is not affected by steroid timing.

MS-associated optic neuritis

Female:male ~3:1, age 20-50, peak 30s. ON is the presenting feature of MS in ~25% of cases; ~50% of isolated ON develops MS within 15 years. MRI risk stratification: ≥1 demyelinating lesion → 72% MS at 15 years; zero lesions → 25% at 15 years.

Most patients regain near-normal VA within 4-6 weeks regardless of steroid. Persistent deficits in color and contrast are common. Disposition: outpatient infusion if available; neurology referral for disease-modifying therapy discussion regardless of MRI burden.

NMOSD

Red flags over MS-ON: severe loss (no light perception or hand motion only), bilateral or rapidly sequential, poor recovery, age >50 or non-white ethnicity, longitudinally extensive transverse myelitis (≥3 vertebral segments), area postrema syndrome (intractable hiccup/vomit). Confirm with AQP4-IgG by cell-based assay (NOT ELISA — older ELISAs are insensitive).

Methylprednisolone
1 g IV daily × 5 days
IV
Longer course than MS-ON.
Add PLEX for severe / steroid-refractory attacks
5-7 sessions every other day, starting at 48-72 h if no improvement, or immediately if severe. Early PLEX (within 5 days of onset) significantly improves outcomes. IVIG (2 g/kg over 2-5 days) is second-line when PLEX is unavailable or contraindicated.

Lifelong immunosuppression once confirmed: rituximab, eculizumab, satralizumab, inebilizumab; mycophenolate/azathioprine are older alternatives. Relapses cause cumulative blindness/paralysis — prevention is everything.

MS drugs WORSEN NMOSD
Interferon-beta and natalizumab (MS drugs) worsen NMOSD — never start an MS disease-modifying therapy without ruling out NMOSD first.

MOG-AD

Often bilateral with prominent disc swelling (papillitis pattern), more common in children and young adults, may follow viral illness or vaccination. Confirm with MOG-IgG by cell-based assay (live cell-based preferred over fixed) — low positive titers in adults may be nonspecific.

Methylprednisolone
1 g IV daily × 3-5 days
IV
Follow with a SLOW oral taper over 4-6 weeks. IVIG or PLEX for severe / steroid-refractory disease.
Fast taper is the most common cause of relapse
Stopping the taper too fast within 6 weeks is the most common cause of MOG-AD relapse in the ED-discharge population. Many patients have monophasic disease and need no maintenance; relapsing course is treated with rituximab, mycophenolate, IVIG, or low-dose maintenance steroid — discuss with neuro-immunology.

Neuroretinitis (macular star)

Disc swelling PLUS a macular star (lipid exudate radiating from the fovea, visible 1-2 weeks after symptom onset) — not a true demyelinating ON, a separate entity.

  • Bartonella henselae (cat-scratch disease) — most common identifiable cause; ask about cat/kitten exposure, scratches, regional lymphadenopathy
  • Syphilis — always rule out with RPR + treponemal test
  • Lyme disease in endemic areas
  • Tuberculosis in endemic areas / risk factors
  • Idiopathic in ~30%

Workup: Bartonella IgM/IgG + PCR, RPR, treponemal test, Lyme serology, TB screen, HIV, ESR/CRP, MRI brain + orbits.

Atypical / expanded workup

Triggering features: bilateral simultaneous, no light perception at nadir, no recovery at 4-6 weeks, severe disc swelling with hemorrhages, age extremes, steroid-non-responsive, recurrent.

  • AQP4-IgG, MOG-IgG (cell-based)
  • ANA, ENA, anti-dsDNA (lupus / connective tissue)
  • ACE, lysozyme, chest CT (sarcoidosis)
  • RPR, treponemal, HIV
  • Bartonella, Lyme (especially with neuroretinitis features)
  • B12, methylmalonic acid, copper, ceruloplasmin (nutritional / metabolic mimics)
  • Paraneoplastic panel (CRMP-5/CV2, ANNA-1/Hu, ANNA-2/Ri) — small cell lung CA, lymphoma
  • CSF (oligoclonal bands, cells, glucose, protein, cytology if malignancy suspected)
  • MRI brain + orbits + cord with gadolinium

Started while the expanded workup pends; PLEX if severe / non-responsive. Specific maintenance therapy is cause-directed. Disposition: neuro-immunology consult or expedited neuro-ophthalmology referral.

Reference: MRI protocol (confirm + stratify)

Order: MRI brain WITH and WITHOUT gadolinium, INCLUDING dedicated orbit sequences with fat-saturation. Timing: within 24-48 hours of presentation; do not delay steroid more than 24 hours waiting for imaging. Send AQP4/MOG before steroid regardless.
