Upper GI Bleed — Resuscitate, Risk-Stratify, Route

Adults ≥18 in the ED with suspected upper GI hemorrhage (hematemesis, melena, or hematochezia from a brisk UGIB) proximal to the ligament of Treitz. Excludes pediatrics and lower GI bleeding. Balloon tamponade/TIPS content is rescue-tier reference, not a routine first step.

⚠ TXA is NOT recommended for GI bleeding — HALT-IT trial: no mortality benefit, increased VTE and seizures.
⚠ Restrictive transfusion: transfuse at Hgb <7 g/dL (target 7-8 g/dL), NOT <9 — except ACS, target >8 g/dL. Villanueva NEJM 2013: 45% relative mortality reduction with restrictive vs liberal transfusion. Over-transfusion raises portal pressure and worsens variceal bleeding.
⚠ Suspected variceal bleeding: start octreotide 50 mcg IV bolus, then 50 mcg/hr BEFORE endoscopic confirmation — do not wait for EGD to start vasoactive therapy.
⚠ Avoid over-resuscitation in variceal bleeding — excess crystalloid/blood increases portal pressure and worsens bleeding; target permissive hypotension (SBP 90-100 mmHg), not normotension.
⚠ Balloon tamponade: INTUBATE FIRST (aspiration risk is high) and obtain a CXR to confirm gastric position BEFORE any inflation. Esophageal balloon pressure must NEVER exceed 45 mmHg (esophageal necrosis/rupture risk) and inflation is capped at 24 hours with deflation every 6-8h.
⚠ Airway obstruction from tube migration is an emergency: CUT ALL LUMENS at the bifurcation and remove the entire apparatus immediately.

2 · Cirrhosis check — pathway split

Variceal bleeding accounts for 10-20% of all UGIB, but in cirrhotic patients with GI bleeding: 59% variceal, 16% peptic ulcer disease, 25% other causes.[6]

Cirrhosis/variceal suspected — start vasoactive therapy now
Start octreotide immediately, before EGD confirmation. Restrictive transfusion is critical — over-resuscitation raises portal pressure and worsens bleeding. Antibiotics reduce mortality in cirrhosis. Target mild hypotension, not normotension.[6][12]
Non-cirrhotic — non-variceal workup
Peptic ulcer disease is the leading cause (35-50%); erosive gastritis and Mallory-Weiss each account for 8-15%. BUN:Cr ratio >30 suggests an upper source.[1][2]
