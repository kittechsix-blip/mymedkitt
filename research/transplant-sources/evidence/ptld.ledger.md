# PTLD (post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder) in adult solid organ recipients: evidence ledger

The transplant-specific infectious-disease guideline chapter on PTLD/EBV (AST IDCOP 2019, e13652) and the adult BSH PTLD guideline were NOT obtainable (ACQUISITION-2026-11-21.md). Everything below is what the saved documents say; nothing comes from outside knowledge. Compiled 2026-11-21.

## How to read the rows

- One claim per row, 25 words or fewer. QUOTE is verbatim (line-break hyphens, ligatures and Unicode hyphens normalised; "..." marks an omission inside one passage). The claim keeps the source verb (recommend, suggest, should, consider, may).
- **SOURCE keys:** KDIGO = KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for the Care of Kidney Transplant Recipients, Am J Transplant 2009;9(Suppl 3) (PDF page = printed S-page + 13). ISHLT-H = ISHLT guidelines for the care of heart transplant recipients, J Heart Lung Transplant 2023;42(5) (PDF page = printed e-page). AASLD-NG = AASLD/AST practice guideline on adult liver transplantation, non-graft-related complications, Liver Transpl 2025 (HTML, no page numbers; locator = guideline Question/section). ISHLT-L07 = Stewart 2007 lung rejection nomenclature, J Heart Lung Transplant (printed page). AST-PNA = AST IDCOP pneumonia in SOT, Clin Transplant 2019;33:e13545 (PMC7162188; HTML, locator = section). KIR26 = "Cytomegalovirus: Updated Approach to Management for Kidney Transplant Recipients" (Kidney Int Rep 2026, PMC13697321; a review). REV21 = Lindsay/Hirsch et al., EBV PTLD update on management and outcomes, Curr Opin Infect Dis 2021 (PMC8589110; REVIEW). REV24 = Dharnidharka, PTLD, Semin Nephrol 2024 (PMC11213680; REVIEW). ZHONG = Zhong, Approach to transplant infectious diseases in the ED, Emerg Med Clin North Am 2018 (PMC6237280; REVIEW, SOT and HCT together). IPTA26 = IPTA Nashville consensus update on PTLD in children, Pediatr Transplant 2026 (PMC13560100; PAEDIATRIC). Labels = FDA SPL in dailymed/.
- **GRADE** exactly as printed. KDIGO: Level 1 "recommend"/Level 2 "suggest", A to D. ISHLT: Class and Level of Evidence. AASLD: (Strong/Weak, Level n; n=1 highest). "rationale" or "ungraded" = narrative text carrying no grade. "label" = FDA label.
- **TAG:** NODE (short bedside line) | OVERLAY (detail) | TEAM (what the transplant team does) | DOSE (contains a dose; none here).
- **IS** = the row names reducing, holding, stopping or changing an immunosuppressant (to carry a "call the transplant team" parenthetical later). Index at the end of the rows.
- Tables and numbers (KDIGO Table 29 p S82, KDIGO 13.3 box p S48, ISHLT e72 and e22 tables, ISHLT e50 table) were read from rendered page images. HTML sources have no page image; they were read from the text.
- Populations: all rows are adult unless POPULATION says otherwise. KDIGO is a kidney guideline; AASLD is liver; ISHLT-H is heart (adult and paediatric mixed, labelled per row).

## SOURCES DIFFER

- **How often does reducing immunosuppression alone work?** KDIGO 2009 (P11): "as many as two thirds" respond; less likely after 1 year or with lymphoma (P12), yet reduction "may still be considered even in these patients, though expectations of efficacy will be reduced" (P57). Curr Opin review 2021 (P52): response from reduction alone is "low"; add rituximab or T-cell therapy. Semin Nephrol review 2024: response 43 to 63% in retrospective studies. Do not state one number.
- **EBV load and antivirals.** KDIGO (P3): monitor D+/R- kidney recipients (2C). ISHLT 2023 heart (P24): insufficient evidence to support antiviral prophylaxis for PTLD prevention in D+/R-; pre-emptive EBV load monitoring should be considered (Class IIa, C). AASLD 2025 liver (P28): serial EBV PCR screening not supported (Weak, Level 3); a markedly high load can flag risk (P31). KDIGO itself says the load is sensitive but not specific (P5) and also that a compatible syndrome with a high load is a sensitive and specific approach to diagnosis (P59), with caution about asymptomatic elevations.
- **Mortality.** KDIGO (P10): over 50% (2009, observational). AASLD (P33): survival above 50% at 10 years. Not reconcilable from the text; keep out of the page.

## NOT IN THE SOURCES READ

1. **When to suspect PTLD, and an ED red-flag list.** No guideline gives one. Nearest sourced fact: P30 (nonspecific fevers, night sweats, weight loss, bulky lymphadenopathy; AASLD), P8 (EBV disease as febrile illness, gastroenteritis or hepatitis; KDIGO). Reviews (framing only): P44, P46, P47, P65 (up to 40% of infections present without fever).
2. **When to image and with what.** Not in the guidelines read. Nearest: P30 (imaging may show bulky lymphadenopathy), P36/P37/P38 (lung PTLD, nodules, biopsy). Review (Semin Nephrol 2024) says imaging, especially with radiation, should be reserved for high suspicion (P48) and identifies lesions to biopsy (P67).
3. **Which tests to send from the ED.** Not stated. Nearest: P5, P6, P59, P60 (EBV load limits; tissue may be the only way to confirm or exclude), P9 (biopsy imperative; KDIGO).
4. **When to admit.** Not in the guidelines read; a review (emergency medicine, 2018) says emergency physicians should have a lower threshold to admit transplant patients with serious infection for specialised inpatient evaluation and management including adjustment of immunosuppression (P64); it is about serious infection, not PTLD. Nearest guideline facts: P13, P21 (transplant-centre evaluation).
5. **How to adjust immunosuppression in the ED for suspected PTLD.** Not in the guidelines read. Nearest: P1, P2, P14, P16, P17, P57, P58 (reduction is the established treatment once EBV disease or PTLD is diagnosed, with the rejection trade-off); P21, P13 (transplant centre decides). Heart target-level text for PTLD exists for children only (P62).
6. **PTLD in lung, pancreas, intestine and multi-organ recipients.** No guideline saved; lung documents are pathology only (P39); SRTR registry reports are not guidelines and are not used. Nearest: P36, P37.
7. **Central nervous system presentations.** No ED guidance. Nearest sourced facts: P40 (belatacept label: consider PTLD with new neurological, cognitive or behavioural signs), P61 (ISHLT lists CNS PTLD among causes of seizure or mental status change), P66, P67 (reviews).
8. **Treatment regimens beyond reduction and rituximab, and prognosis.** Not for the ED. Nearest: P22, P34, P52.
9. **Documents searched with no PTLD content:** EASL 2024 liver transplantation guideline (its only lymphoma mention is donor-derived cancer), AASLD/AST graft-related 2024 and candidate evaluation 2025, ISHLT lung CLAD/AMR/ALAD/BLAD/GAP documents, CMV consensus 2025 and BK consensus 2024 (EBV appears only in a T-cell therapy table, a methods sentence and references).

## USE RULES

- P1 and P2 must not appear without P21/P13 (the decision belongs to the transplant team) and must name that KDIGO is a kidney guideline with grades 1C and 2D.
- P5, P6 and P59 must show together; P49, P60, P71 only with them.
- P10 is a 2009 observational figure; never alone and never set beside P33 as if current.
- P11 must keep "As many as" and must never appear without P12 and P57.
- P23 is a statement about solid tumours unrelated to the lymphoid system; show it only with P21/P22 and never as an instruction to reduce immunosuppression for any cancer.
- P24 must be worded "insufficient evidence to support antiviral prophylaxis", not "antivirals do not prevent PTLD".
- P27 population unclear (adult or child); show only as "ISHLT heart guideline, IIb, C" with the stated scope.
- P55, P56, P62, P63 are paediatric; never on an adult page.
- P40 to P43 and P69 are label risk statements, not ED instructions.
- Reviews (P44 to P54, P64 to P68, P71) supply framing only; no review row may carry an instruction, a threshold or a disposition rule.
- KDIGO 2009 is older; ISHLT 2023 (P22) is newer on rituximab. Say "older" for KDIGO.

## SUFFICIENCY

A useful, honest ED page can be composed from GUIDELINE and LABEL rows, but it is short and mostly about recognition and handoff, not management. It could say: PTLD is a lymphoma-spectrum complication of immunosuppression with nonspecific presentation (fever, night sweats, weight loss, lymphadenopathy, bowel, lung or CNS masses); EBV-seronegative recipients and belatacept-treated recipients carry higher risk (P4, P30, P31, P40, P41); EBV blood level can mislead either way (P5, P6, P59, P60); diagnosis needs tissue read by an experienced pathologist (P9); once diagnosed, reduction of immunosuppression is the established first step, with rituximab or chemotherapy by the transplant centre (P1, P17, P22, P34, P21); reduction is the transplant team's call (P23, P13). It cannot say when to image or what to send, what to give or hold in the ED, when to admit for PTLD, how PTLD looks in lung, pancreas or intestinal recipients, or what finding should prompt a call. Those gaps come from the unobtainable AST 2019 PTLD chapter and the absence of any ED guideline.
