# Induction and maintenance regimens in adult transplant recipients: evidence pack

Source of record: OPTN/SRTR 2024 Annual Data Report, chapters Kidney, Liver, Heart, Lung, Pancreas (srtr.hrsa.gov/adr/2024). Saved as stripped text: `SRTR_2024_ADR_Kidney_text.txt`, `..._Liver_text.txt`, `..._Heart_text.txt`, `..._Lung_text.txt`, `..._Pancreas_text.txt`. Adults only; pediatric sections ignored.

## How to read the locators and the limits of the saved text

- Saved text files, no page numbers. Locator = file + line number in the saved file + section heading as printed. Quotes are verbatim from the saved text.
- **The percentages come only from the narrative sentences.** The figures themselves (Figure KI 47-49, LI 52-54, HR 51-53, LU 43-45, PA 37-38) were stripped as images; only their captions survive. No figure data table for any induction or regimen figure is in the saved text (searched for tacrolimus, mycophenolate, basiliximab, thymoglobulin, alemtuzumab, belatacept, sirolimus, everolimus, cyclosporine: none appear outside the narrative sentences below). So any figure value not quoted below is **UNREADABLE (not in saved text)**.
- **Decimal-point check:** every percentage quoted below was re-read in the saved text and is intact (decimal points present). Arithmetic cross-checks: kidney induction types 76.8 + 16.0 + 1.5 = 94.3 (matches the stated total); lung 85.0 + 2.6 + 1.0 = 88.6 (matches); pancreas 72.2 + 23.1 = 95.3 vs stated 95.4 (rounding difference, see Ambiguities). No percentage was found split or garbled, so no figure is marked UNREADABLE for garbling; missing figures are marked "not in saved text".
- **Timing as the report words it:** the reports' figure captions say "Immunosuppression at transplant reported to the OPTN" (induction) and "Immunosuppression regimen at transplant reported to the OPTN" (regimen). The narrative says "during the initial transplant hospitalization period" (kidney), "discharged on" (heart), "at the time of transplant hospitalization discharge" (pancreas). **No 1-year regimen data are in any saved adult chapter** (no sentence or caption reports immunosuppression at 1 year). All regimen figures below are at transplant/discharge.
- **The reports do not name individual induction agents.** They name only classes: "T-cell-depleting agents" (TCD), "interleukin-2 receptor antibody" (IL2Ab / "interleukin-2 agents"). Brand/generic names (thymoglobulin, basiliximab, alemtuzumab) are not used in the adult narrative. Pancreas reference list contains a title "Thymoglobulin induction decreases rejection in solitary pancreas transplantation" (Pancreas line 63) but that is a citation title, not report data.
- **Use of "triple therapy":** the reports DO use it in some chapters (see each organ). Verbatim uses: Kidney "use of triple therapy including steroids"; Lung "triple-drug therapy"; Heart "triple immunosuppressive therapy". Liver and Pancreas adult text do not use the phrase.
- Figure captions define: "MMF, all mycophenolate agents; Tac, tacrolimus." Scope in every caption: "All adult ... transplants, including retransplant and multiorgan."

## 1. Kidney (Kidney file, section 2.2 Transplants, line 41)

**1.1 Induction share.**
> "Induction immunosuppression was used in 94.3% of kidney transplants in 2024"
> "T-cell-depleting induction agents alone, accounting in 2024 for 76.8% of recipients, while 16.0% received interleukin-2 receptor antibodies (IL2Ab) alone, and 1.5% were reported as receiving both agents"
(Figure KI 47/KI 48 values read from the narrative only.)

**1.2 Regimen at transplant.**
> "Most patients received tacrolimus and mycophenolate-based regimens during the initial transplant hospitalization period, with use of triple therapy including steroids and use of tacrolimus-mycophenolate (without reported steroid use) in 70.4% and 23.0% of recipients, respectively"
So: tacrolimus + mycophenolate + steroids ("triple therapy including steroids") 70.4%; tacrolimus + mycophenolate without reported steroids 23.0%. The sum of the two is 93.4%; the remaining 6.6% is not described in the saved text.

**1.3 At 1 year:** not in saved text.

**2.1 Induction share.**

**3.1 Induction share.**

> "interleukin-2 agents have been more common than T-cell-depleting agents (28.4% versus 19.3% in 2024), and dual-agent therapy has remained very rare"

## 4. Lung (Lung file, section 2.2.2 Outcomes of transplant recipients, line 61)

**4.1 Induction share.**
> "Induction agents were used in 88.6% of recipients in 2024, up from 56.9% in 2013"
> "Interleukin-2 receptor antibody (IL2Ab) agents alone accounted for 85.0% of all induction therapies administered, followed by T-cell-depleting agents at 2.6%, and combined IL2Ab plus T-cell-depleting agents at 1.0%"
(Denominator wording: "85.0% of all induction therapies administered"; the three figures sum to 88.6%, equal to the overall induction use, so they behave as percentages of all recipients, but the report's phrase is ambiguous, see Ambiguities.)

**4.2 Maintenance at transplant.**
> "Maintenance immunosuppression continued to be dominated by triple-drug therapy, with tacrolimus, mycophenolate, and steroids used in 87.9% of regimens"
The report uses "triple-drug therapy". Remaining regimens: **UNREADABLE (not in saved text)**.

## 5. Pancreas (Pancreas file, section 3 Transplants, line 47)

**5.1 Induction.** Reported **by center**, not by recipient:
> "The use of lymphodepleting induction regimens continued to increase and was the dominant agent in 2024, with 91.4% of centers reporting using this strategy. Only 1.9% of centers reported using interleukin-2 receptor antibodies alone for induction and 5.4% did not use any induction therapy in 2024"
The percentage of recipients receiving induction is **not in saved text**. The report says "lymphodepleting" (Figure PA 37 caption uses "TCD, T-cell depleting").

**5.2 Maintenance at discharge.**
> "Almost all maintenance regimens at the time of transplant hospitalization discharge (95.4%) incorporated tacrolimus and a mycophenolate agent (MMF) in 2024, with 72.2% of these using steroids as well (up from 65.5% in 2023). In 2024, of the maintenance regimens using tacrolimus and MMF, 23.1% were steroid free, down from 30.6% in 2023"
So: tacrolimus + MMF 95.4% of regimens; of these, 72.2% with steroids and 23.1% steroid free. The report does not use "triple therapy" in the adult pancreas text. Regimens without tacrolimus + MMF (4.6%) not described.

**5.3 At 1 year:** not in saved text.

## Ambiguities

- Whether "steroid" means maintenance prednisone is not defined in the text; the report says "steroids"/"steroid"/"corticosteroids" only.
- Kidney/liver "tacrolimus-mycophenolate" arms are described as "without reported steroid use": absence of a report, not confirmed steroid-free.
- Pancreas percentages are of regimens/centers, not recipients; 72.2% + 23.1% = 95.3% versus the stated 95.4% (rounding).
- Lung induction wording "85.0% of all induction therapies administered" sums with 2.6% and 1.0% to 88.6%, the same figure as overall induction use, so they appear to be shares of all recipients; the report does not say so explicitly.
- Heart regimen is "discharged on" (discharge), kidney is "initial transplant hospitalization period", figure captions say "at transplant": these are the report's three phrases for the same OPTN data field; whether they are identical is not stated.
- Report year: figures are "in 2024" transplants for induction and regimens; outcome statistics in the same chapters use earlier cohorts.
