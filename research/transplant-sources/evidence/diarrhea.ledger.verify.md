- First-line CDI treatment 2017 vs 2021: strengths stated correctly (strong/high vs conditional/moderate; vancomycin remains acceptable). Missing from the 2021 update, same page: Rec II recurrent CDI "we suggest fidaxomicin (standard or extended-pulsed regimen) rather than a standard course of vancomycin (conditional recommendation, low certainty)", with tapered/pulsed vancomycin acceptable for a first recurrence; Rec III bezlotoxumab for a recurrence within 6 months (conditional, very low), comment: patients with primary CDI and risk factors "such as age >=65, immunocompromised host [per history or use of immunosuppressive therapy], and severe CDI on presentation may particularly benefit". The 2021 update says nothing on severe or fulminant episodes, so D34 stands alone; the ledger says this correctly.
- C. difficile frequency differs: IDSA/SHEA 2017 SOT prevalence 7.4%, 5-fold general medicine (D26); Zhong 2018: "C. difficile infection affects up to 30% of SOT patients, often with severe disease." Not listed.
- IDSA 2017 rec 9 says blood cultures "should be obtained from ... people who are immunocompromised (strong, moderate)" but the rationale says "Blood cultures may be considered in immunocompromised people who are febrile". Not listed; D16 must not stand alone.
- Other bullets (empiric antimicrobials, MMF reduction, gut CMV, loperamide) accurate.
