# tpe-cytopenia evidence ledger: low blood counts on a transplant drug

Page: `tpe-cytopenia` in consult "Transplant Patient in the ED". Built ledger-first: every row is a short claim, a verbatim quote and a locator; nothing here comes from outside the sources. Compiled 2026-11-20. No doses are given as instructions (a trial dose appears once, marked).

## How to read this ledger

- **CLAIM** is 25 words or fewer and keeps the source's strength ("should", "may", "consider", "not recommended"). A scope prefix names the document. **QUOTE** is 40 words or fewer; `...` joins two stretches of the same passage. Both limits and the quote text were machine-checked (see the check line at the end).
- **Labels** were read from the saved SPL XML. Section numbers are the label's own. Labels carry no grades (shown as n/a). A label's "should be avoided" or "not recommended" is the label's wording, not a guideline grade.
- **KDIGO 2009** was read from the PDF text layer, not from rendered page images (the text layer was clean). Locators give PDF page and journal page. Recommendations 22.1 to 22.3 are printed "Not Graded". Everything else quoted from KDIGO is rationale text, ungraded. In the text layer the symbol µ prints as "l" ("/lL"). Table 31 (which drug causes which count) has a two-column layout that the text layer scrambles, so no row relies on its columns.
- **CMV 2025** is a PMC HTML page with no page numbers. Locators are the section heading plus the paragraph tag [P#] used in `evidence/cmv.md`. The grade "(strong, moderate)" is printed once at the end of the three-sentence leukopenia statement, so it is shown on all three of its rows.
- **USE tags:** SUMMARY-CANDIDATE (could open the page), NODE (page body), OVERLAY (per-drug table), TEAM (change to an immunosuppressant or antiviral; the page shows it with "(call the transplant team first)"), APP (fact about the app).
- The EM review (Zhong 2018) is a narrative review, not a guideline. It is the only saved source that says what to give a transplant patient with sepsis or neutropenic fever, and it covers SOT and HCT together. Its rows are labelled as such.

- **Verification round (2026-11-20):** 29 rows were corrected in place (ids unchanged) and 25 rows were added at the end (C154 to C178), so ids cited in the verify report still point to the same rows. A USE of "none" means the row is not for the page (background or review-level framing only).

## SOURCES DIFFER

Same decision, different instruction. Both are quoted; neither is chosen.

**1. Valganciclovir or ganciclovir when counts are low (labels vs CMV consensus 2025, treatment of CMV disease)**

- Valcyte label 5.1 (C34): "VALCYTE should be avoided if the absolute neutrophil count is less than 500 cells/µL, the platelet count is less than 25,000/µL, or the hemoglobin is less than 8 g/dL". The ganciclovir label says "not recommended" at the same counts (C46).
- CMV consensus 2025, treatment of CMV disease (a statement graded strong, moderate; C118, C120): "In the setting of leukopenia, attempts should be made to continue the use of valganciclovir or ganciclovir." and "We do not recommend reducing the dose of valganciclovir or ganciclovir based on leukopenia alone". It names no count.
- The consensus statement sits in the list for treating CMV disease; it does not address prophylaxis, where the labels' counts also apply.

**2. Myfortic threshold and verb inside one label**

- Myfortic 5.6 (C12): "neutropenia develops (ANC < 1.3 × 103/mcL) or anemia], dosing with Myfortic should be interrupted or the dose reduced".
- Myfortic section 10 (C16): "neutropenia with absolute neutrophil count < 1.5 x 103/mcL or anemia), it may be appropriate to interrupt or discontinue Myfortic".
- The number (1.3 vs 1.5) and the verb (interrupt or reduce vs interrupt or discontinue) both differ. CellCept uses 1.3 (C1).

**3. Allopurinol with azathioprine (Imuran label vs KDIGO 2009)**

- Imuran label (C28): "Patients receiving IMURAN and allopurinol concomitantly should have a dose reduction of IMURAN ... Concomitant use of IMURAN and febuxostat is not recommended".
- KDIGO 2009 rationale (C117; ungraded; the sentence carries reference 616, an oncology CSF review): "The combination of allopurinol and azathioprine should be avoided to prevent neutropenia".

**4. G-CSF for transplant recipients (KDIGO 2009 vs CMV consensus 2025 vs labels)**

- KDIGO (C113): "there is potential for harm with treatment. One case report suggested that CSFs may have been associated with worsening graft function"; and "There are not likely to be any RCTs to determine when to give CSFs in KTRs" (C116).
- KDIGO also, from oncology evidence (C114, C115): "patients with febrile neutropenia (absolute neutrophil count <500/µL ...) benefit from CSFs along with antibiotics if there is pneumonia, fungal infection, hypotension, sepsis syndrome or multisystem organ failure"; "inadequate data" for afebrile neutropenia below 1000/µL.
- CMV consensus, CMV-disease treatment (C121; ungraded): "The use of colony-stimulating factors has been shown to be safe and could be considered". Valcyte label (C38): "hematopoietic growth factors may be considered".
- Scope differs: KDIGO is about neutropenia in kidney recipients; CMV and the labels are about valganciclovir or ganciclovir cytopenia.

**5. Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole and neutropenia (Imuran label vs KDIGO 2009)**

- Imuran label (C29): "co-trimoxazole, may lead to exaggerated leukopenia, especially in renal transplant recipients".
- KDIGO rationale (C108; ungraded): "The risk of neutropenia from trimethoprim–sulfamethoxazole in KTRs is unclear" (TMP-SMX alone). The Bactrim label lists agranulocytosis and aplastic anemia (C54).

**6. What to adjust first when counts fall (KDIGO 2009 vs CMV consensus 2025 vs ISHLT heart)**

- KDIGO (C110; ungraded): "probably reduction of the dose of MMF or azathioprine, are the first suggested actions in case of anemia or leucopenia".
- CMV consensus, CMV-disease treatment (C121): "discontinuation of other myelosuppressive therapies should be tried".
- ISHLT heart, 2010 recommendation continued 2023 (Class I, C; C130): "reduction of bone marrow suppressive agents if leucopenia occurs" (non-lymphoid solid tumors; a stretch for this decision).
- None ranks which drug to reduce first.

**7. Persistent neutropenia on valganciclovir prophylaxis (Valcyte label vs AASLD/AST 2025 vs CMV consensus 2025)**

- Valcyte label (C34): avoid at ANC <500/µL.
- AASLD/AST 2025, liver (Strong, Level 2; C127): "letermovir can be substituted for valganciclovir if there is persistent neutropenia from valganciclovir prophylaxis".
- CMV consensus 2025's leukopenia statement covers treatment of CMV disease only, so no source here gives a prophylaxis rule for leukopenia that matches either.

**8. Blood-count schedule (KDIGO 2009 vs labels)**

- KDIGO 22.1, Not Graded (C167, C168): at least: daily for 7 days or until hospital discharge, two to three times per week in weeks 2 to 4, weekly in months 2 to 3, monthly in months 4 to 12, then at least annually.
- CellCept (C8: consider), Myfortic (C166: should be performed) and Imuran (C165: suggested): weekly in month 1, twice monthly in months 2 and 3, then monthly (CellCept and Myfortic through the first year; Imuran, or more often if therapy changes). Dapsone (C64): weekly for month 1, monthly for six months, then twice a year.

**9. Cyclosporine and low counts (labels and KDIGO)**

- Cyclosporine labels (C95): "Cyclosporine does not cause bone marrow suppression in animal models or man". The Neoral adverse-reaction table nevertheless lists leukopenia (C177).
- KDIGO (C104; ungraded): "CNIs are not generally associated with leucopenia".
