## Head-misuse assessment (exact label sentences; same in Valcyte Table 9, Par/Fresenius Table 6)

- Cyclosporine or amphotericin B: "Monitor renal function ... because of potential increase in serum creatinine." Toxicity KIDNEY; "Adds kidney risk:" faithful (Par W&P 5.2 adds: creatinine rose in elderly and transplant recipients; "monitoring renal function ... is essential").

Reading "Adds kidney risk: zidovudine" is wrong: zidovudine and flucytosine are marrow drugs, and the label never assigns.
- Probenecid: "Ganciclovir dose may need to be reduced. Monitor for evidence of ganciclovir toxicity." (up-arrow ganciclovir). "Raises level:" faithful. Didanosine: up-arrow didanosine, "closely monitored for didanosine toxicity (e.g., pancreatitis)". "Raises the other drug:" faithful.
