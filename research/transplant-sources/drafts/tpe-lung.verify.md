A reader in a lung recipient meets "severe immunosuppression" and "not a transplant candidate" wording with no recipient branch.
- Sepsis default (`sepsis-abx-empiric`): beta-lactam backbone, one of pip-tazo 4.5 g q6h, cefepime 2 g q8h ("antipseudomonal"), meropenem ("reserve for MDR risk"); vancomycin only if MRSA risk factors (soft tissue, line/hemodialysis, recent hospitalization/long-term care, IVDU, known colonization, nosocomial); vancomycin stopped at 48 h if no MRSA. Transplant is not a listed MRSA risk factor. `sepsis-special` adds for the immunosuppressed "Broader empiric coverage (consider fungal, PJP, atypical organisms)". ALAD p13: guided by local microbiology surveillance and the infectious history, "particular consideration for coverage against resistant organisms such as Staphylococcus aureus and Pseudomonas aeruginosa", empiric therapy "can be challenging or even contraindicated" with fungal colonization and MDR organisms. Pseudomonas cover is in the default sepsis backbone; S. aureus/MRSA cover is conditional on risk factors that do not include transplant; local surveillance and recipient culture history are not mentioned.
- Pneumonia default: non-severe inpatient CAP = ceftriaxone + azithromycin (or levofloxacin), no antipseudomonal or MRSA agent; Pseudomonas risk list: prior respiratory isolation, bronchiectasis, severe COPD, tracheostomy, abscess/empyema, recurrent pneumonia admissions; MRSA risk list: prior isolation, IV antibiotics or hospitalization in 90 days, long-term care, ESRD, IVDU, empyema, recent influenza. Prior isolation criteria could apply to a recipient but transplant itself is not a trigger. HAP/VAP regimens include antipseudomonal beta-lactams. No antifungal in any default. pna-start asks "Is the patient immunocompromised?" with no branch.
- HFNC: one transplant line in `hfnc-immunocompromised`, "Post-transplant: Consider infection control precautions" (no conflict).
- PE hub: Wells/PERC/D-dimer chain; ALAD says nothing on rules in recipients (finding 9).
