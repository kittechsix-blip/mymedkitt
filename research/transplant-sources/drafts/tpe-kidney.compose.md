# tpe-kidney compose map

Every sentence on the page (node summaries, node bodies, recommendation, critical actions, and the five overlays) is listed with the ledger rows it restates (`evidence/kidney.ledger.md`, ids K1-K383; N1-N17 = the NOT IN KDIGO items; X1-X9 = the DIFFERS items). A sentence restates a row's CLAIM with its scope and grade; rationale and background rows carry `(rationale)` or `(background)`; ungraded recommendations carry `(not graded)`. Other ids: H = ledger header (the guideline's own date, as of March 2009); EN1, EN2 = the two fixed editor notes; P = a link to a page of this consult; R = a Related consults line (plain links, two-word reasons, no clinical statement); L-ind, L-inh, L-ccb = tacrolimus label statements as recorded in the cni-levels evidence pack (not KDIGO); A-uti, A-hiv = App content lines (consult named only); V = the verification report's note that 14.2.3 and 14.2.4 are not reconciled.

Reduce-immunosuppression wording: every sentence that names reducing, withdrawing, stopping, replacing or switching an immunosuppressant carries (call the transplant team first) in the same sentence.

Must-not-appear-alone rows used: K250 (rifabutin) and K238 (ketoconazole, calcium-channel blockers), each only in the drugs overlay beside the tacrolimus label statement. K104-K106, K109, K111-K114, K116, K117, K120 and K196 are not used; the infection node links to the BK and CMV pages instead. Required pairings: K14 with K17 and K18 (overlay, same section); K166 with K167 (infection node and overlay); K91 with K98 (infection overlay); K144 keeps its split grade; K180 keeps some experts and K150 keeps 2D.

Bullets are built from the ledger CLAIM text with the grade or (rationale) marker taken from the row's GRADE field; no verb, grade or population was changed.
