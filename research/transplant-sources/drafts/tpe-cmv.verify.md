## 4. Stated conflicts

[2]: >=100,000 IU/mL is inside the Strong, Level 1 statement; 50,000-100,000 is "many centers favor", ungraded narrative. Both quoted correctly, attributed correctly.
- Resistance exposure 4 vs 6 weeks: both values are quoted correctly but BOTH are from [1], and they answer different questions (item 7). Grades: 4 wk is (strong, moderate) in treatment and (strong, high) in resistance; the page uses only "strong, moderate" and "more than" (resistance section says "minimum 4 wk"). 6 wk is ungraded narrative and carries a qualifier the page drops.
- Foscarnet markers: [2] ">20,000–50,000 IU/mL" (Strong, Level 1; "refractory disease and high viral load") correct. [1] "≥50,000 IU/mL" correct number and "expert opinion" correct, but the page drops "no specific threshold can be defined ... many experts suggested ... may be useful" (item 8).
- Conflict the page does NOT show: treatment endpoint and secondary prophylaxis (item 6).
