## 5. Editor notes (5 in bodies: 2 in tpe-cni-htn-agents, 3 in tpe-cni-htn-dispo; plus CRITICAL_ACTIONS[4])

2. agents and dispo and CRITICAL_ACTIONS[4] (same sentence three times): "call the transplant team before changing, holding or stopping any immunosuppressant; the heart guideline's PRES statement is for heart recipients, and no source read here gives a blood pressure number, a rate of lowering or an agent for high blood pressure in a recipient on a calcineurin inhibitor." (c)/(d): pre-empts the Medication Guide sentence "Do not stop taking or change your dose ... without talking to your healthcare provider"; duplicated three times; and its second clause is false (finding 2). Keep one, correct the clause.
3. dispo: "tell the transplant team about headache, visual change, confusion or a seizure with high blood pressure, because the heart guideline and the cyclosporine labels link PRES to hypertension." (a) practical; "because" is sourced and true for those two sources (tacrolimus labels do not link PRES to hypertension; page says "heart guideline and the cyclosporine labels", accurate).
4. dispo final: "Editor note: disposition is decided with the treating transplant team." required.
Is it too many? The page has 5 notes against 1,500 words; the repeated "call" note is the one to cut. None smuggles an unsourced clinical claim except the clause in finding 2.
